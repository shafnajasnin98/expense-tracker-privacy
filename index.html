Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Finora Expense Tracker handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is managed.

Information We Collect

The app can be used without creating an account. However, if you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, basic personal information such as your name, email address, profile photo, and unique user ID is securely processed through Google and Firebase Authentication solely for login and profile display purposes.

We do NOT store your financial data on external servers.

Financial Data

All income, expenses, bank names, card references, and other financial records entered into the app are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Bank accounts or card details added within the app are for personal record-keeping only. Finora is not a bank and does not connect to financial institutions, process payments, or access real bank accounts.

Analytics

This app uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how users interact with the app and to improve performance and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain information such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data and interactions
  • General location information (such as country or region)
  • Device identifiers

This data is collected in an aggregated manner and is not used to personally identify users. Firebase Analytics does not collect or track any financial information entered in the app.

How Your Information Is Used

  • To authenticate your account when signing in with Google
  • To display your profile information inside the app
  • To analyze app usage and improve features
  • To maintain app stability and performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Limited personal information may be securely processed by trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob

These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Authentication is handled securely by Google Firebase. We do not store your login credentials.

Your financial data remains fully under your control and can be deleted at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the application.

Advertising

Finora Expense Tracker uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information to provide personalized and non-personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • App interaction data
  • General location information (such as country or region)

This information is processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policies:

https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Finora does not access or use your financial records for advertising purposes. Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through their device settings.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Account Deletion

Finora allows users to sign in using Google Sign-In or use the app in Guest Mode. If you signed in using Google and wish to delete your account, you can request deletion by contacting us at:

jasnideveloper@gmail.com

  • Please include:
  • Subject: Delete Finora Account
  • The Google email address used to sign in
  • What happens after deletion:
  • Google Sign-In access will be revoked
  • Finora does not store financial data on external servers
  • All expense data is stored locally on your device
  • To completely remove all data, uninstalling the app is sufficient
  • Deletion requests are processed within 7 days

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
